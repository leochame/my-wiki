# AI Writing Index

AI writing is split by the kind of article being written.

## Subfolders

- `engineering-practice/` for hands-on implementation, workflows, evals, and agent-building articles
- `theory-and-foundations/` for conceptual explanations and fundamentals
- `frontier-papers/` for paper notes, reviews, and article-style interpretations of recent research

## Placement Rules

- Put practical guidance and experience sharing into `engineering-practice/`.
- Put conceptual explanations into `theory-and-foundations/`.
- Put paper-driven pieces into `frontier-papers/`.
