# Writing Index

This area is for original writing that is meant to become publishable or at least coherent long-form output.

## Main Branches

- [[writing/technical/INDEX|Technical writing]] for programming and engineering articles
- [[writing/personal-reflections/INDEX|Personal reflections]] for essays outside pure software topics
- [[writing/idea-garden/INDEX|Idea garden]] for raw writing ideas, outlines, and half-formed article seeds
- [[writing/script/INDEX|Scripts]] for publishable scripts, including short-form video content
- `novals/` for novel drafts and fiction writing
- [[writing/system-design/INDEX|System design]] for architecture deep-dives and walkthroughs

## Placement Rules

- If the piece is meant to become an article, it belongs in `writing/`.
- If the piece is meant to become a script, it belongs in `writing/script/`.
- If it is still a vague thought or seed, put it in `idea-garden/`.
- If it is mainly for interview preparation rather than publishing, consider `job/` instead.

See [[writing/technical/INDEX|Technical Writing Index]] for the technical article taxonomy.
