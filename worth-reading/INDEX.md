# Worth Reading Index

This area tracks articles, papers, and resources that are worth reading later.

## Subfolders

- `by-topic/` for reading queues grouped by topic
- `reading-notes/` for notes created after reading
- `finished/` for curated finished-reading summaries if needed later

## Placement Rules

- Keep the queue concise and list-oriented.
- Create standalone reading-note files only when you actually extract insights.
- Do not mix your own article drafts into this section; those belong in `writing/`.
