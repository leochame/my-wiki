Net debt = SBG net interest-bearing debt
•

Other adjustments: the sum of adjustments of (d) to (j) below

f.
Deduction of the amount equivalent to the outstanding balance of the borrowings made through margin loans backed by Arm shares.

h. Deduction of financial liabilities related to prepaid forward contracts (collar contracts) backed by T-Mobile shares.
i.
Deduction of financial liabilities related to prepaid forward contracts (collar contracts) backed by Intel shares.
j.
Inclusion of the outstanding balance of the loan using OpenAI shares held by SVF2 and guaranteed by SBG.

LTV = Net debt / Equity value of holdings
