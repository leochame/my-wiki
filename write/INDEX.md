## Write / Articles Index

This folder is the **write** area for my longer-form pieces: technical articles and life essays.  
Each subfolder holds one kind of writing, and individual articles live as standalone `.md` files inside.

### Folder map under `write/`

- **`pre-ai-tech/`**: Classic CS & engineering topics before the AI era  
  (distributed systems, backend engineering, databases, operating systems, networks, infrastructure, and other core tech topics)
- **`idea/`**: Raw ideas and sparks for future work  
  (things I want to do, topics I might write about, half-baked outlines, and quick dumps of inspiration)
- **`works/ai-practice/`**: Hands-on AI / LLM practice  
  (practical experience, workflow summaries, troubleshooting notes, tutorial-style guides)
- **`works/ai-theory/`**: Conceptual and theoretical pieces about AI  
  (principles, methods, frameworks, and high-level abstractions about AI)
- **`works/system-design/`**: System Design & interview-oriented design notes  
- **`works/ai-frontier-papers/`**: Notes and reviews of frontier AI papers  
  (deep-dive reading notes, reviews, and personal interpretations of cutting-edge AI research)
- **`works/life-article/`**: Life, learning, and reflection essays  
  (writing about life, habits, learning methods, career reflection, and other non-purely-technical topics)
- **`随笔/`**: Free-form personal notes and non-article records  
  (random thoughts, small records, fragments, and other things that are not full “articles”)

When creating a new article, choose the closest folder above and add a new `.md` file there;  
the assistant should never treat this index as a database, only as a simple map of the writing structure.
