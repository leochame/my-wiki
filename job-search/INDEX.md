# Job Search Index

Use this area for everything directly related to getting a job.

## Subfolders

- `resumes/` for resume versions and supporting material
- `interview-preparation/` for interview notes, answer banks, mock prep, and interview-oriented writing
- `companies/` for company-specific research
- `role-research/` for role comparisons and targeting strategy
- `application-tracking/` for application records
- `notes/` for general job-search notes that do not fit a narrower bucket

## Placement Rules

- Put interview practice and answer material in `interview-preparation/`.
- Put company-specific notes in `companies/`.
- Put role and hiring-market reasoning in `role-research/`.

Open this file first before scanning deeper.
