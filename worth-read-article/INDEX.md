## Worth Reading Index

This folder tracks articles and papers worth reading, grouped by their main type of value.  
The current structure is: **one `.md` file per category**, and inside each category file multiple articles are recorded as separate sections (no per-article files).  
All category `.md` files live under the `by-category/` subfolder alongside this `INDEX.md`.

Categories (conceptual only; files are all in this same folder):
- **Pre-AI Tech** — Classic CS/engineering topics (distributed systems, backend, databases, OS, networks, etc.)
- **AI Practice** — Hands-on AI best practices and high-value practical resources
- **AI Theory** — AI theory, principles, and conceptual understanding
- **AI Frontier Papers** — Frontier AI papers and cutting-edge research
- **Tech Progress** — General technology and industry progress
- **Life Experience** — Life, career, and personal growth related reading

Status values:
- **Unread** — Not started
- **Reading** — Currently reading
- **Finished** — Completed (add short notes)

Priority:
- **High** — Should read soon
- **Medium** — Useful but not urgent
- **Low** — Nice to have
